# Code of conduct

I want Vacuula for Home Assistant to be useful and welcoming to people with
different backgrounds, experience levels and Home Assistant setups. I ask
everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and other project spaces to
treat each other with respect.

## Taking part

- Be patient and constructive. Describe the problem and the evidence.
- Give specific feedback and leave room for people to learn.
- Respect different levels of technical experience and access to hardware.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Ask before sharing another person's logs, screenshots or device information.

I do not accept harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, repeated
disruption or publishing private information without permission. Credentials,
tokens, private addresses, robot serial numbers and private Home Assistant
configuration do not belong in public reports.

## Reports and moderation

I review conduct concerns raised in project issues or pull requests. If a
report needs private information, ask me to arrange private contact before
sharing the details. For a security vulnerability, use the private reporting
process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

I may remove comments, close discussions, limit participation or take other
reasonable action when conduct harms people or the project. This policy
applies to the repository and any spaces that represent this project.
